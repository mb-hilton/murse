What Is a Murse?

A murse is any bag with one strap — where the strap is defined as anything that can fit over the head. A handle does not count as a strap.

✔ A murse must have:
  • Exactly one strap
  • A strap that fits over the head
  • Wearability on the body (crossbody, shoulder, chest, waist)
✘ Not required:
  • Gendered styling
  • Specific size
  • Specific material
